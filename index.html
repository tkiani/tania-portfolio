01 — Introduction

A fashion designer who shapes stories into garments — from the first thread of research to the final look on the runway.

I'm Tania Zahid Kiani, a fashion designer and content creator working across conceptual collections, print, textiles and South Asian craftsmanship. I had the honour of presenting my work at Graduation Fashion Week 2026, and first walked my designs down the runway at university fashion shows in 2021 — moments that shaped how I design for both the audience and the individual.

Alongside the catwalk, my work experience spans hands-on design and production — pattern cutting, garment construction, techpack development and digital embroidery — giving me the technical fluency to take a concept from first sketch to a finished, production-ready piece. It's design with narrative depth, backed by real industry craft.
