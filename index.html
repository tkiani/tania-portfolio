01 — Introduction

A designer who builds worlds — from the first thread of research to the final image on the feed.

I'm Tania Kayani, a fashion designer and content creator working across conceptual collections, print, textiles and South Asian craftsmanship. My practice moves fluidly between the atelier and the algorithm — I design garments with narrative depth and then translate them into content that reaches an audience.

Each collection begins with a concept and is carried through with technical rigour: draping, embroidery, print development and material research. The result is work that is both story-driven and production-ready — the balance brands look for.
